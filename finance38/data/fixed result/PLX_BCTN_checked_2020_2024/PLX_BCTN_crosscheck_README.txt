Decisions used:
- State ownership was coded from the direct holding of "Ủy ban Quản lý vốn nhà nước tại doanh nghiệp" shown in the BCTN ownership table.
- Institutional ownership was coded from the "Tổ chức" line in the BCTN ownership table.
- Foreign ownership was coded from the "Nước ngoài" line in the BCTN ownership table.
- Managerial ownership was coded from the "Người nội bộ và người có liên quan của Người nội bộ" line.
- Number of employees was verified from the note stating "Tại ngày 31 tháng 12 năm ..., Tập đoàn có ... nhân viên."
- R&D expenditure was kept at 0 because the annual reports / financial statements do not separately disclose a monetary R&D expense line.
- Product innovation was kept at 1 for all years, but this is an inference from annual-report evidence on cleaner-fuel / green-energy / EV-charging / product-transition initiatives. This is weaker than a directly disclosed "product innovation" checkbox.
- Process innovation was kept at 1 for all years and is strongly supported by annual-report evidence on digital transformation, automation, cashless payment, e-invoice, BI, PLX-ID, and operating-system upgrades.
