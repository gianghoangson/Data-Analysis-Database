PNJ BCTN cross-check notes (2020-2024)

What was rechecked against the uploaded annual reports (BCTN):
1) Ownership / disclosure rows:
   - 1 Managerial ownership
   - 2 State ownership
   - 3 Institutional ownership
   - 4 Foreign ownership

2) Binary / disclosure-sensitive rows:
   - 18 R&D expenditure
   - 19 Product innovation
   - 20 Process innovation

3) 36 Number of employees

4) 38 Firm age

Rules used:
- State ownership was coded from the BCTN shareholder tables. PNJ shows 0.00% state ownership in all uploaded BCTN years.
- Foreign ownership was coded from the 'Trong đó: Nước ngoài' line in the shareholder-structure table.
- Managerial ownership was coded from 'Người nội bộ' in the 'Cơ cấu cổ đông theo loại cổ phiếu' table for 2021-2024.
- For 2020, the uploaded BCTN does not show the same aggregate 'Người nội bộ' table on the ownership page, so managerial ownership was computed from the year-end insider holdings table in the governance section, excluding the foreign-investor representative rows and excluding 'người có liên quan' rows.
- Institutional ownership was coded as organization ownership from the 'Cơ cấu cổ đông theo loại cổ phiếu' table:
    domestic organizations + foreign organizations + company union.
  This overlaps with foreign ownership by design.
- 2020 institutional ownership could not be verified cleanly from the uploaded 2020 BCTN because that report only shows the domestic/foreign shareholder table and not the same organization-vs-individual ownership split that appears in later years. I left row 3 blank for 2020 instead of forcing a guess.
- Number of employees was verified from the note stating 'Tại ngày 31 tháng 12 năm ..., Tập đoàn có ... nhân viên.'
- Firm age was computed from 1988 as the founding year shown in the company history pages:
    2020=32, 2021=33, 2022=34, 2023=35, 2024=36.
- R&D expenditure was set to 0 because the uploaded BCTN does not separately disclose a monetary R&D expense line.
- Product innovation was coded 1 for all years because the BCTN shows clear product / brand / collection development evidence:
    2020 launch of Style by PNJ; later years continue with new collections / brand expansion / CAO Bespoke / product-category development.
- Process innovation was coded 1 for all years because the BCTN shows strong process / system / digital-transformation evidence:
    2020 F5 refresh and operating redesign; 2021 ERP/RMS-mPOS/CDP and broader digital transformation; later years continue supply-chain, factory, management-system, and digital / operating upgrades.
